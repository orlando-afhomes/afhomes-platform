# Finance Sales and Commissions Rule-to-Code Map

Reviewed 2026-11-11 against the local `develop` working tree at `d3c0b18`, including existing local edits. The platform behavior below describes the in-memory mock implementation. This map records findings and proposed automation for owner review.

A payment can support three different decisions. **Confirmed Sale** uses the finalized contract's required payment and the date both conditions are satisfied. **Closed Deal** uses the workbook's product threshold and the date on the crossing receipt. **Commission payout** uses the 40% net verified threshold and a seven-day wait from Finance verification. The same receipt can contribute to all three.

## Verification cases for the next implementation

- A finalized ₱100,000 VIP contract has a ₱40,000 required initial payment. A counted ₱25,000 receipt dated November 2 and verified November 5 would qualify the sale as a Closed Deal dated November 2 under the workbook rule. A further ₱15,000 verified November 10 confirms the sale and reaches the payout threshold on November 10. Payout opens November 17 if the other conditions hold.
- A submitted receipt and a returned receipt contribute zero before verification. Retrying the same receipt or adjustment must leave balances and reported totals unchanged after the first successful application.
- A linked refund removes workbook Closed Deal qualification even if remaining collections exceed 25%. If that same adjustment causes the required contractual payment to fail, document 13 separately governs the Confirmed Sale reversal and its reporting period.
- A payout crossing with no traceable verification date must not enter the ready queue or be recordable once the missing-date handling is corrected.

Use the payment ID, contract and sale IDs, actor, timestamp, reason and before/after state to trace each verification, adjustment, and resulting sales or commission event. If Closed Deal is adopted, retain the crossing receipt, threshold applied and later changes in the audit history so a reported figure can be reproduced.
